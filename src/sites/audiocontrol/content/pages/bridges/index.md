---slot: title

Hands on the box. Eyes on the room.

---

---slot: intro

Hardware sits beautifully on the desk; the DAW does what the DAW does
well. A bridge is a small piece of native software that lets a vintage
box drive a modern session — transport, jog, locate, mixer — without
giving up the editor on the screen.

---slot: closingLabel

Why bridges

---

---slot: closing

The best old hardware was built for the hands. Buttons under your
fingertips, a data wheel, a screen the size of a postage stamp because
you don't need a big screen when the controls are this clear. The DAW
brought modern editing power and gave away that tactile vocabulary.
Bridges put it back — without asking you to leave the DAW.
