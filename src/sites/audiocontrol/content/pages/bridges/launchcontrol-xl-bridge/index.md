Make LUNA's mixer hands-on.

---slot: heroBody

The Novation LaunchControl XL mk3 (2024) is a USB controller built for the
mixer — twenty-four endless rotaries in three rows of eight, eight 60mm
faders, sixteen buttons, RGB feedback on every control. The bridge maps it
to LUNA's mixer surface. Move a fader, the channel level moves. Twist a
knob, the send moves. Hit a button, the track mutes. LUNA doesn't ship
LaunchControl support out of the box. The bridge does.

---slot: capabilitiesLabel

What it does

---

---slot: capability1Tag

Faders

---

---slot: capability1Title

Eight faders, eight channels.

---

---slot: capability1Body

LCXL fader → LUNA mixer fader. 60mm of throw, 7-bit MIDI resolution. Move it on the surface, the channel moves on the screen.

---

---slot: capability2Tag

Knobs

---

---slot: capability2Title

Three rows for pan and the sends.

---

---slot: capability2Body

Twenty-four endless rotaries cover the channel strip — pan on top, send 1 in the middle, send 2 on the bottom. Eight tracks live at once.

---

---slot: capability3Tag

Buttons

---

---slot: capability3Title

Mute and solo per channel, RGB-lit.

---

---slot: capability3Body

The two button rows are the moves you reach for first when the mix is shifting under your hands. RGB feedback shows what's armed without taking your eyes off the room.

---slot: installLabel

Install

---

---slot: installStep1

[Download the disk image](https://github.com/audiocontrol-org/audiocontrol/releases?q=launchcontrol-bridge) from the audiocontrol GitHub releases. The release tag begins with `launchcontrol-bridge`.

---

---slot: installStep2

Open the DMG and drag the application into `/Applications`.

---

---slot: installStep3

Launch the app, point LUNA at it as a hardware control surface (Mackie Control protocol), and you're running.
