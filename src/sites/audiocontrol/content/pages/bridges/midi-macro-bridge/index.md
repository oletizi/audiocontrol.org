---slot: heroBody

The Roland MC-500mkII (1988) is a sequencer made for the hands —
eight tracks of MIDI, a data wheel, 99 location memories, and a
screen the size of a postage stamp because the controls are this
clear. The bridge turns it into a hardware control surface for a
modern DAW. You hit play on the box, the DAW plays. You spin the
wheel, the DAW jogs. You recall a location, the DAW seeks.

---slot: capabilitiesLabel

What it does

---

---slot: capability1Tag

Transport

---

---slot: capability1Title

Play, stop, record, locate from the box.

---

---slot: capability1Body

The MC-500's transport buttons drive the DAW transport. Eyes on the
room, hands on the buttons your fingers already know.

---

---slot: capability2Tag

Jog

---

---slot: capability2Title

The data wheel becomes the jog wheel.

---

---slot: capability2Body

Spin the wheel; the DAW scrubs. The same gesture you've used for
decades — re-pointed at a modern session.

---

---slot: capability3Tag

Locate

---

---slot: capability3Title

99 location memories as jump-to-marker buttons.

---

---slot: capability3Body

Pre-configure verse, chorus, bridge as MC-500 locations. One press
on the console and the DAW seeks. Or punch a bar number into the
keypad — the DAW teleports there. Years of muscle memory finally
paying rent.

---slot: installLabel

Install

---

---slot: installStep1

[Download the disk image](https://github.com/audiocontrol-org/audiocontrol/releases?q=midi-macro-bridge) from the audiocontrol GitHub releases. The release tag begins with `midi-macro-bridge`.

---

---slot: installStep2

Open the DMG and drag the application into `/Applications`.

---

---slot: installStep3

Launch the app, point your DAW at it as a hardware control surface (Mackie Control protocol), and you're running.

---slot: reportLabel

If it breaks

---

---slot: reportBody

Got an MC-500mkII (or an MC-50) and a supported DAW? Try it. If it breaks on your setup, [file an issue](https://github.com/audiocontrol-org/audiocontrol/issues/new) with what gear, what DAW, and what you saw. The protocol details — MIDI Song Position, Mackie Control, the bridge architecture — are [on GitHub](https://github.com/audiocontrol-org/audiocontrol).
