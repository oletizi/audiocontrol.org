# Article: "Coding Agents Are Insane, Hyperintelligent Toddlers"

The opener of the stackcontrol.org **"Building deskwork"** devlog series. A first-person
account of arriving at a process for agentic development: from hand-coding audio DSP and
sampler SysEx control, to building browser editors *with* agents (and inventing the
guardrails by necessity), to generalizing it into the **dw-lifecycle** plugin, and now
rebuilding it as **stack-control** on Spec Kit — keeping the audit barrage and scope
discovery.
