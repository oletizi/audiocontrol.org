# The Lifecycle, and Why Agents Need One

The series opener / thesis. Frames every later piece.

Angle: unstructured agentic work drifts — it skips scope, over-builds, and ships unreviewed. The lifecycle puts gates between intent and merge.

Key beats:
- The five phases: define → workplan → implement → audit → ship.
- What each phase buys you (scope captured up front; tasks backed by issues; commits at boundaries; cross-model audit; PR with operator-owned merge).
- The "control plane" framing — the lifecycle as a surface that watches work move through phases.
- Contrast with vibes-coding: where it breaks down and why structure wins.
