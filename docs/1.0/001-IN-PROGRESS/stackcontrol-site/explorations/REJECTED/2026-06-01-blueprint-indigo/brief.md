# Blueprint — indigo/violet refined technical schematic

## What

A drafting-table schematic identity: deep indigo-tinted near-black under a faint dotted blueprint
grid, a single indigo/violet accent (`255 78% 70%`), Syne (geometric display) + Hanken Grotesk
(body) + JetBrains Mono. Signature device was the lifecycle rendered as a labeled node-and-edge DAG
(with an Audit → Implement loop-back edge), reinforced by crosshairs and registration corner marks.
More generous negative space and a more refined, elegant register than the chosen Telemetry
direction.

## Why rejected

It was the strongest runner-up — genuinely elegant, and the DAG-with-loop-back captured the
lifecycle's one non-linear edge (audit sends you back to implement) better than the accepted
direction's straight rail. It lost on *register fit*: the refined drafting-table aesthetic read as a
documentation/reference site, slightly too quiet for a product page that also wants to feel like a
live tool. The Telemetry direction's denser, dashboard-like control-plane was a closer match to what
deskwork actually is. Recorded here so the indigo/schematic direction is not re-proposed; its DAG
loop-back idea is worth revisiting if the phase rail ever needs to express the audit→implement
return visually.

## When

Retired 2026-06-01 at the Phase 2 operator pick, in favor of Telemetry (see the ACCEPTED entry).
