# Stack — magenta bold typographic z-depth

## What

The boldest of the three directions: near-black with a single electric-magenta accent
(`322 90% 64%`), Martian Mono used *as the display face* for giant headlines and outlined phase
numerals (01–05), and the "stack" metaphor made literal through layered, progressively offset cards
with hard z-depth shadows. Maximalist-leaning, loud, grid-breaking.

## Why rejected

The most memorable and the most opinionated — but opinion cut against the audience. The target
reader is a developer evaluating a tooling plugin; the maximalist magenta-and-z-depth treatment read
as a launch/marketing page rather than a builder-to-builder instrument, and risked feeling louder
than the product warrants at this stage. It also strained the family's "restrained, instrument-like"
discipline more than the siblings do. Magenta cleared the differentiation bar (neither amber nor
chartreuse), so the rejection is about register, not hue. Recorded so the magenta/maximalist
direction isn't re-proposed; the giant-numeral phase treatment could be salvaged as an accent on a
future surface without adopting the whole maximalist frame.

## When

Retired 2026-06-01 at the Phase 2 operator pick, in favor of Telemetry (see the ACCEPTED entry).
